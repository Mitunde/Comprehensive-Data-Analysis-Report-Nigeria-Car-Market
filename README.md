# Comprehensive-Data-Analysis-Report-Nigeria-Car-Market
This report combines visual dashboard insights and tabular data extracted from the Nigerian Car Market dataset (source: Car45). It summarizes vehicle performance metrics, brand popularity, regional distribution, color preferences, and transmission choices, providing a full picture of Nigeria’s automotive landscape.

## Data Understanding
The dataset originates from Car45.com, containing records of car listings and sales across different Nigerian states. It captures:
- Vehicle characteristics (price, horsepower, engine size, mileage)
- Brand and model information
- Transmission type
- Color preferences
- Geographic distribution

# Data Analysis and Key Insights
 ## Market Structure

- The Nigerian car market is import-driven, dominated by foreign-used cars (Tokunbo).

- Toyota is the clear market leader due to its affordability and availability of parts.

- Luxury segments (Lexus, Mercedes-Benz) show steady but limited growth.

## Regional Trends

- Lagos remains the country’s automotive capital — home to ports, car dealers, and a dense buyer population.

- Abuja ranks second with premium car demand (luxury focus).

- Southern states (Oyo, Edo, Rivers) are emerging secondary markets.

# Consumer Behavior

- Nigerians prefer reliable, affordable, mid-range SUVs and sedans.

- Automatic transmission (94%) reflects modern driving convenience.

- Black, White, and Silver cars dominate resale and style trends.

# Performance Insights

- Vehicles typically have moderate mileage and engine size, indicating fairly new imports.

- Average engine power (209 HP) is consistent with compact and crossover vehicles.

## Economic and Policy Implications

- Foreign exchange rates and import duties heavily impact pricing.

- Encouraging local assembly plants could reduce costs and improve accessibility.

- High-demand zones like Lagos and Abuja should guide dealership expansion strategies.

# Conclusion
The Nigerian car market is vibrant yet concentrated, dominated by Toyota and primarily driven by urban buyers in Lagos and Abuja.
With average prices at ₦4 million and a strong preference for automatic transmission vehicles, the market reflects a shift toward convenience, reliability, and value retention.
Opportunities exist for policy reforms, dealership expansions, and tech-driven insights to improve transparency and affordability in the automotive sector.
